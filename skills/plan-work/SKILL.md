# Plan Work

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Start from the approved outcome and acceptance criteria.
2. List all required deliverables and derive milestones from them.
3. Order dependencies, expose parallel work, and mark the critical path.
4. Assign owners only when supplied or authorized; otherwise use role placeholders.
5. Attach a verification method and completion evidence to every milestone.
6. Surface risks, decision points, and the first immediately executable actions.

## Deliver

- Scope and assumptions
- Milestones and deliverables
- Dependencies and parallel tracks
- Owners or role placeholders
- Risks and decisions
- Verification and next actions

## Quality gates

- Cover the full outcome rather than optimizing only the easiest part.
- Keep at most one active action per owner unless concurrency is intentional.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
