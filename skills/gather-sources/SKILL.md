# Gather Sources

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Define the question, decision context, freshness requirement, and exclusions.
2. Search primary and authoritative sources before summaries or commentary.
3. Record title, publisher, date, URL or file location, and the claim each source can support.
4. Diversify sources when the question benefits from independent perspectives.
5. Deduplicate near-identical sources and flag inaccessible or stale evidence.
6. Stop when the decision-relevant claims have adequate coverage; record remaining gaps.

## Deliver

- Research scope
- Source ledger
- Coverage by question or claim
- Freshness and authority notes
- Gaps and search limits

## Quality gates

- Never cite a search result page when the underlying source is available.
- Distinguish publication date from the date an event occurred.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
