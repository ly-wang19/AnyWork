# Make Decision

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Restate the decision, decision owner, deadline, and constraints.
2. Summarize options and decision-relevant evidence without replaying all research.
3. Recommend one option or explicitly recommend delaying when evidence warrants it.
4. Explain benefits, costs, risks, reversibility, and opportunity cost.
5. State assumptions, dissenting views, trigger conditions, and stop conditions.
6. Define the owner, next action, review date, and evidence to monitor.

## Deliver

- Decision statement
- Recommendation
- Evidence and trade-offs
- Risks, dissent, and uncertainty
- Triggers and follow-up

## Quality gates

- Do not imply authority the user has not granted.
- Make the recommendation falsifiable and reviewable.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
