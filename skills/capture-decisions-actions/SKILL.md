# Capture Decisions and Actions

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Identify the meeting scope, participants, and source boundaries.
2. Separate discussion, proposal, decision, action, risk, and open question.
3. Capture exact decisions with rationale and source location.
4. Record owner and due date only when explicitly stated or later confirmed.
5. Merge duplicate actions and expose contradictions.
6. Produce a follow-up record that participants can correct quickly.

## Deliver

- Decisions and rationale
- Actions, owners, and due dates
- Open questions and blockers
- Risks and parking lot
- Source references

## Quality gates

- Never infer an owner or deadline from who spoke most.
- Mark tentative language as proposed rather than decided.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
