# Clarify Outcome

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Restate the requested outcome without adding facts.
2. Identify audience, deliverable, deadline, constraints, dependencies, and non-goals.
3. Separate known facts, assumptions, and unresolved questions.
4. Ask only questions whose answers would materially change the work; otherwise state a reversible assumption.
5. Write measurable acceptance criteria and the first executable next action.

## Deliver

- Outcome and audience
- Deliverable and format
- Constraints, dependencies, and non-goals
- Acceptance criteria
- Assumptions and open questions
- Next action

## Quality gates

- Do not invent deadlines, owners, budgets, or authority.
- Keep the contract short enough for the requester to approve or correct.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
