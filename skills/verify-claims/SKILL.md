# Verify Claims

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Split compound statements into atomic, testable claims.
2. Classify each claim as factual, interpretive, predictive, or value-based.
3. Find the strongest available supporting and contradicting evidence.
4. Record source, date, exact support, limitations, and conflicts.
5. Assign supported, contradicted, mixed, or unverified with calibrated confidence.
6. Propose a precise correction for unsafe or overstated wording.

## Deliver

- Claim ledger
- Verdict and confidence
- Supporting and contradicting evidence
- Limitations and conflicts
- Recommended correction

## Quality gates

- Do not convert absence of evidence into evidence of absence.
- Keep quotations short and preserve their original meaning.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
