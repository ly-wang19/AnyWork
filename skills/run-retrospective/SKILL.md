# Run Retrospective

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Define the period, intended outcome, actual outcome, and evidence sources.
2. Build a neutral timeline and separate facts from interpretations.
3. Identify enabling and constraining system factors without personal blame.
4. Distill lessons that transfer beyond the single event.
5. Choose a small set of improvement experiments with owner, date, and measure.
6. Schedule a follow-up to verify whether the actions changed outcomes.

## Deliver

- Outcome comparison
- Evidence timeline
- What helped and hindered
- Causes and lessons
- Owned improvement experiments
- Follow-up date and measure

## Quality gates

- Do not rewrite uncertain memories as facts.
- Prefer testable system changes over generic commitments.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
