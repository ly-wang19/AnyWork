# Draft Document

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the document type, audience, decision, length, and house style.
2. Create an outline that maps every brief requirement to a section.
3. Draft the core argument before polishing introductions or summaries.
4. Support material claims and label assumptions or recommendations.
5. Check transitions, internal consistency, terminology, and cross-references.
6. Revise against the brief and acceptance criteria before delivery.

## Deliver

- Purposeful title and summary
- Complete logical structure
- Evidence-backed body
- Risks, limitations, or alternatives
- Actionable conclusion

## Quality gates

- Do not add unsupported facts to make the draft sound complete.
- Make the document easy to review and revise.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
