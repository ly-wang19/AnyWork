# Write Message

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Confirm sender, recipient, relationship, channel, purpose, and desired response.
2. Separate verified facts from explanation, request, and commitment.
3. Lead with the information most relevant to the recipient.
4. State action, owner, timing, and response path explicitly.
5. Match formality, directness, length, and cultural register to the audience.
6. Review for ambiguity, accidental promises, sensitive data, and emotional impact.

## Deliver

- Subject or opening when appropriate
- Context and key message
- Requested or proposed action
- Owner and timing
- Courteous close

## Quality gates

- Draft by default; send only when the user explicitly authorizes the exact target and text.
- Do not fabricate urgency, authority, shared history, or commitments.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
