# Design Presentation

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Confirm audience, occasion, decision, duration, slide count, and visual constraints.
2. Write the narrative arc and takeaway for each slide before layout.
3. Use evidence to support one primary message per slide.
4. Choose visuals that reveal relationships rather than decorate.
5. Keep titles assertive, labels readable, and sources visible.
6. Render and inspect every slide for overflow, contrast, density, and continuity.

## Deliver

- Narrative arc
- Slide-by-slide messages
- Accurate evidence and visuals
- Speaker guidance when requested
- Rendered quality check

## Quality gates

- Do not shrink text to rescue an overloaded slide.
- Do not use a chart when a sentence or small table is clearer.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
