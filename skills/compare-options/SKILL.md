# Compare Options

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the decision, must-haves, exclusions, and acceptable trade-offs.
2. Define non-overlapping criteria and explain weights before scoring.
3. Gather comparable evidence for every option and mark missing data.
4. Apply one scoring scale consistently; keep facts separate from judgment.
5. Test whether reasonable weight changes alter the ranking.
6. Highlight dominant trade-offs, uncertainty, and verification needed.

## Deliver

- Decision criteria and weights
- Comparable evidence matrix
- Scores and rationale
- Sensitivity analysis
- Trade-offs and recommendation conditions

## Quality gates

- Do not reward an option for missing evidence.
- Do not conceal a failed must-have inside a weighted average.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
