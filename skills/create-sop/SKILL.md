# Create SOP

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Define purpose, scope, users, prerequisites, inputs, outputs, and authority.
2. Observe or reconstruct the happy path at executable detail.
3. Add decision points, exceptions, stop conditions, and escalation routes.
4. Attach quality checks, evidence, records, and completion criteria.
5. Separate policy requirements from implementation suggestions.
6. Walk through the SOP as a new operator and remove ambiguity.

## Deliver

- Purpose and scope
- Roles and prerequisites
- Numbered procedure
- Branches and exception handling
- Quality controls and records
- Escalation and version ownership

## Quality gates

- Do not hide permissions or irreversible actions inside a routine step.
- Use stable identifiers and examples without embedding secrets.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
