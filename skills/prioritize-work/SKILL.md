# Prioritize Work

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the objective, capacity, horizon, and mandatory commitments.
2. Normalize each item to a clear outcome and comparable level of detail.
3. Choose criteria, scales, and weights appropriate to the decision.
4. Score with evidence and explicit uncertainty.
5. Apply dependencies, sequencing, and must-do constraints after scoring.
6. Publish now, next, later, and not-now groups with reasons.

## Deliver

- Objective and constraints
- Prioritization method
- Ranked and grouped work
- Dependencies and capacity fit
- Reasons for not-now items
- Review triggers

## Quality gates

- Do not treat urgency as value or a score as certainty.
- Keep dependency constraints from being overruled by arithmetic.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
