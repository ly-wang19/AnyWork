# Prepare Meeting

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Define the meeting outcome and test whether a meeting is necessary.
2. List decisions, inputs, attendees, roles, and required pre-work.
3. Create a time-boxed agenda ordered around decisions, not updates.
4. Prepare a concise pre-read with evidence and explicit questions.
5. Set facilitation, note-taking, decision, and escalation roles.
6. Plan for missing information, absent decision-makers, and time overruns.

## Deliver

- Meeting objective
- Decisions and questions
- Attendees and roles
- Time-boxed agenda
- Pre-read and preparation
- Fallback and follow-up plan

## Quality gates

- Do not invite people without a clear role.
- Move status updates to asynchronous pre-reading when possible.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
