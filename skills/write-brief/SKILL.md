# Write Brief

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Identify the intended reader, decision, and desired action.
2. Select only evidence that changes understanding or action.
3. Write the context, objective, insight, proposal, constraints, and next step.
4. Use headings and language appropriate to the reader's expertise.
5. Keep claims traceable and mark unresolved assumptions.
6. Compress repetition while preserving decision-critical details.

## Deliver

- Audience and decision
- Context and objective
- Key evidence or insight
- Proposal and non-goals
- Constraints, risks, and next action

## Quality gates

- Keep the brief skimmable and self-contained.
- Do not turn unresolved assumptions into facts.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
