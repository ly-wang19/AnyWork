# Review Deliverable

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Recover the intended outcome, audience, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
2. Inspect the complete artifact and its rendered or executable form when relevant.
3. Identify defects with precise evidence and distinguish severity from preference.
4. Check factual support, reasoning, consistency, completeness, and usability.
5. Prioritize fixes by impact and provide the smallest effective remediation.
6. Re-verify changed areas and adjacent risks after revision.

## Deliver

- Overall verdict
- Acceptance-criteria coverage
- Prioritized findings with evidence
- Concrete remediation
- Residual risks and verification

## Quality gates

- Do not praise or criticize without evidence.
- Do not silently implement fixes when the request is review-only.

## Language

- Follow the user's latest explicit output-language instruction.
- Otherwise answer in the dominant language of the request: English, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
