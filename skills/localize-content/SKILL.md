# Localize Content

Produce an evidence-aware, directly usable work result. Keep facts, assumptions, and recommendations distinct.

## Workflow

1. Confirm source locale, target locale, audience, region, channel, and purpose.
2. Extract non-negotiable facts, terminology, names, numbers, links, and legal text.
3. Adapt structure, idiom, politeness, date and number formats, and cultural references.
4. Preserve intended effect rather than sentence order.
5. Back-check facts, omissions, terminology, placeholders, and links.
6. Flag untranslatable, legally sensitive, or culturally risky material for review.

## Deliver

- Localized content
- Terminology decisions
- Material adaptations
- Unresolved risks or reviewer notes

## Quality gates

- Never translate code, identifiers, citations, or brand names unless authorized.
- Use business-appropriate Japanese register and audience-appropriate Chinese and English.

## Language

- Produce the localized artifact in the explicitly requested target language and locale, even when the request itself uses another language.
- Use the user's requested output language for explanations and reviewer notes; otherwise use the dominant language of the request.
- If the target language or locale is missing and would materially change the result, ask for it or state a reversible assumption before localizing.
- Preserve quoted source text, code, identifiers, product names, and citations in their original language unless translation is requested.
- Match the audience's register; distinguish formal and conversational Chinese, business and plain Japanese, and the requested English variety.
